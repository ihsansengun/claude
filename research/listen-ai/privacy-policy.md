# Narrata Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-14 · Theory of Web · narrata@theoryofweb.com

## Summary

Narrata does not collect personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your documents and your reading progress stay on your device and, if you enable iCloud, in your private iCloud account.

## What the app processes, and where

| Data | Where it is processed | Leaves your device? |
|---|---|---|
| Documents you import (PDF, EPUB, text, scans) | On your device | No |
| Web articles you add by link or share sheet | Fetched directly from the website you chose, on your device | Only the request to that website |
| Photos of pages you scan | Text recognition on your device (Apple Vision) | No |
| Speech (built-in voices) | On your device (Apple speech synthesis) | No |
| Previews, "Simplify" and summaries | Apple Intelligence on your device, or Apple's Private Cloud Compute for long documents | Only to Apple's Private Cloud Compute, which Apple designs so that the data is not stored and is not accessible to Apple or to us |
| Reading preferences and progress | On your device; iCloud sync if you turn it on | Only to your own iCloud account |
| Purchases (Narrata Plus) | Apple's App Store (StoreKit) | Apple processes the purchase; we receive no personal details |
| Cloud voices (Narrata Plus, when available) | Text of the passage being read is sent to the voice provider to generate audio | Yes, the text only; it is not stored by us |

## What we do not do

- We do not run analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
- We do not use advertising identifiers or any form of tracking.
- We do not sell, share or transfer data to third parties.
- We do not require an account or an email address.

## App Store privacy label

Data Not Collected.

## Children

Narrata is rated 4+ and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Your choices

- Delete a document in the Library to remove it from your device.
- Turn iCloud sync off in Settings › [your name] › iCloud.
- Manage or cancel Narrata Plus in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.
- Deleting the app removes all local data.

## Changes

We will update this page and the effective date if anything changes. Material changes will be mentioned in the app's release notes.
