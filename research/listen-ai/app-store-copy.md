Narrata reads your documents aloud and highlights every word as it goes, so you can get through PDFs, books, articles and study notes anywhere: on the train, on a walk, or when your eyes are tired.

LISTEN TO ANYTHING YOU'D READ
• PDFs, EPUB books, Word exports and plain text
• Web articles via the share sheet or a pasted link
• Photos of printed pages, scanned with the camera
• Pasted text from anywhere

FOLLOW ALONG, YOUR WAY
• Word-by-word and sentence highlighting
• Speed from 0.75× to 3×
• Skip by sentence or paragraph, sleep timer, Lock Screen controls
• Fonts, sizes, letter and line spacing, five high-contrast themes
• Focus mode dims everything except the sentence being read

UNDERSTAND MORE
• A three-bullet preview before you start
• "Simplify" rewrites a hard paragraph in plain language
• Summaries of whole documents
All of this runs with Apple Intelligence on your device or on Private Cloud Compute, never on third-party servers.

BUILT FOR EVERY READER
Narrata was designed with readers who have dyslexia, ADHD and low vision. It supports VoiceOver, Voice Control, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion, Increase Contrast and Differentiate Without Color. Highlights use shape as well as colour. Every accessibility feature is free, always.

WORKS WITH SIRI AND SHORTCUTS
"Continue listening in Narrata." "Search Narrata for climate." Add the Continue Listening widget to your Home Screen.

HONEST PRICING
The free version is complete: unlimited listening with the built-in voices, every reading and accessibility feature, and no account. Narrata Plus adds premium cloud voices with a usage meter you can always see, plus unlimited summaries. Monthly or annual only, no weekly plans, cancel any time in Settings.

PRIVACY
Your documents stay on your device. Narrata has no account, no tracking and no analytics SDKs.

**What's new and why it matters (about 1,500 characters):**
Narrata is a new document-to-audio reader built from the ground up for iOS 27. It turns PDFs, EPUBs, web articles and photos of printed pages into a listening library with word-by-word highlighting, and it was designed with and for readers who have dyslexia, ADHD and low vision.

It adopts this year's platform features where they genuinely help: Apple Intelligence through the Foundation Models framework for on-device previews, paragraph simplification and summaries (with Private Cloud Compute for long documents), Siri through App Intents and the system search and open schemas, Core Spotlight indexing, a Continue Listening widget, and the updated Liquid Glass design. It supports every applicable Accessibility Nutrition Label: VoiceOver, Voice Control, Larger Text, Dark Interface, Differentiate Without Color, Sufficient Contrast and Reduced Motion. Highlights use underline as well as colour, and every accessibility feature is free.

We built it because the category's biggest apps charge weekly, hide usage caps and collect data. Narrata has no account, a "Data Not Collected" privacy label, free unlimited listening with system voices, and monthly or annual plans only, with a usage meter that is always visible.

Fifteen readers with dyslexia and ADHD tested the beta; their feedback shaped Focus mode (dim everything but the current sentence) and the sentence-at-a-time audio engine that never stalls mid-document.

## Review notes (App Review)
- No account needed; nothing to log in to.
- Camera is used only for scanning pages (VisionKit). Photos permission is only requested if the user picks a photo.
- Apple Intelligence features degrade gracefully when unavailable; the app is fully usable without them.
- Subscriptions: Narrata Plus monthly / annual, StoreKit 2. Free tier is complete.
